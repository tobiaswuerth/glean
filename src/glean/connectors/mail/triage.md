## Emails

The email comes as Markdown: front matter with its headers (`from`, `to`, `cc`, `date`, `subject`, `direction`,
`attachments`, …), then its text as it came, quoted history and signatures included. `direction` is outbound when the
owner wrote it. The owner's addresses are {owner_addresses}.

Import also an email the owner wrote to a person, however short; a person's words a system brings (a support agent's
reply in a ticket, "Aktualisiert von"); a person sharing something with the owner or inviting them through a platform;
a thread in which the owner answered (their words in the quoted history). Skip notices of comments in a shared document
(Google Docs, Dropbox Paper): the conversation lives there.
